The rules of engagement

Outthink. Outmarch. Outlast.

Capture the opposing army, or leave it with no legal march.

  1. March diagonally. Red advances toward rank 8; blue toward rank 1. A knight moves one open square forward.
  2. Take the opening. Jump an adjacent enemy onto an empty square to capture. When a capture is available, you must take it.
  3. Finish your charge. If the same knight can jump again, continue until no capture remains.
  4. Claim a crown. Reach the far rank to become a king and move or capture in either direction. Crowning ends that turn.
  5. An honorable draw. Three repeated positions or 80 moves without a capture or promotion end in a draw. Either commander may also offer a draw; the rival can accept it, decline it, or let it expire with the next march.

Click a knight, then a gold destination. You can also select a legal move by coordinate below the board. Set either commander to Human, Sparring AI, or External agent.
